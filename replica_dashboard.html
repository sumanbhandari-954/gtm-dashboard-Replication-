Method and sources

  • Bookings and churn: Salesforce Opportunity, CloseDate in 2026, Type ≠ Migration, Deal_ARR__c > 0. Wins use IsWon. Renewal churn is Churn_Amount__c.
  • Plan: quarterly and annual targets parsed from the Reporting Data Warehouse workbook (data/slow/sheet_targets.json, as of 2026-10-27 per data/slow/MANIFEST.json).
  • Beginning ARR: Jan-26 opening ARR of $41.3M from the workbook’s ARR-by-month block.
  • Simple roll-forward = opening ARR + YTD new + YTD upsell − YTD churn. It leaves out renewal uplift, so it does not match the published figure.

Not reproduced yet: NRR and GRR, the Summary verdict, partner channel, Demand Gen and landing pages, Sales Productivity, Pipeline Productivity, Customer Health, Top Accounts and the stage-conversion ladders. Each needs an extra query or a sheet tab that has not been pulled.
